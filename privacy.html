privacy & cookies

last updated 13 November 2026

everly holds words that people trust us to deliver later, so we keep what we collect small and we look after it. here’s the plain version.

who we are

everly (everly.ink) is a London-based message scheduling service. we decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the “data controller”. questions or requests: hello@everly.ink.

what we collect

  • your account: name, email address and password (stored securely, never readable by us).
  • your messages: the words, emojis and chosen template, plus the delivery date. they are secured: encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by account-level access rules so only you can see them, and not released to anyone until the date you chose.
  • your recipients: the name and email address or phone number you give us, so we can deliver your message. please only add people you’re happy to write to.
  • payments: handled by Stripe. we never see or store your card number.
  • delivery records: whether a message was sent, delivered or opened.

why, and our legal basis

  • to run your account and deliver your messages on the date you choose (contract).
  • to take payment and keep financial records (contract and legal obligation).
  • to keep everly secure and prevent misuse (legitimate interests).
  • to measure which of our adverts bring people to everly, only if you say yes to the cookie (consent).

who helps us run everly

we use trusted providers to do this, each only for its own job: Supabase (account and message storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email delivery), Twilio (SMS and WhatsApp delivery), Stripe (payments) and OpenAI (advertising measurement, described below). we don’t sell your data or your messages, and we never read your messages for advertising.

how long we keep things

scheduled messages are kept until they are delivered, or for the holding period of your plan: up to 10 years, or for as long as everly exists on Legacy. you can delete your account at any time and we’ll remove your data, except what we must keep for accounting or legal reasons.

your rights

you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. email hello@everly.ink and we’ll reply within a month. if you’re unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

if we change this page in a way that matters, we’ll update the date above and tell account holders.
